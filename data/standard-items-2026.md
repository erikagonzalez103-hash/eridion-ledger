## Red flags for the audit (visible without cost data)

1. **Same hardware, different prices across items** (old vs new vendor pricing
   coexisting): V1E037 hinge $62.37 vs $90.72 · SGCU1 clamp $15.04 vs $21.82 ·
   S0GC90 $39.04 vs $40.94 · BM6X6 $57.65 vs $61.23 · BCU4 $16.30 vs $16.74 ·
   silicone $13.75 vs $20.00. The 3 Panel Box Shower is priced on the oldest table.
2. **Glass sell $/sqft is inconsistent item to item** (e.g. $12.74–$29.84/sqft for
   the same 3/8" clear tempered) — some lines embed fab, some don't; no single
   derived markup underneath.
3. **Install lines are stale cost-basis numbers**, not current sub flat rates —
   margin on labor is unknown and likely negative on complex items ($72.50 to set
   a 31 sqft heavy panel).
4. **Measure trips billed at 3 different flat rates** (42.78 / 43.50 / 65.25) plus
   an hourly (50.75) for the same activity class.
5. **T&C clause 25** already grants cost-increase repricing rights — unused
   negotiation leverage with builders sitting in Eridion's own contract.
