Luxury Custom Home · Colorback Glass

The tile look, without the grout

Primary Suite Wet Room — New Luxury Build, DFW Metroplex
Scope
Wet room walls, primary suite renovation
Systems
Tempered colorback glass, digitally color-matched to the tile spec
Client
Luxury builder partner + design team
Timeline
1 week, final measure to install

ChallengeThe designer had fallen in love with a specific tile aesthetic for the wet room walls — but the homeowner, experienced with luxury home maintenance, was adamant about not living with grout. Porous grout joints harbor mold and mildew in wet environments and never stop demanding upkeep.

ApproachRather than abandon the design, we fabricated tempered colorback glass panels digitally color-matched to the exact tile shade — the aesthetic the designer loved, in a non-porous surface that meets Glass Association of North America performance standards. Installation was single-stage and fit the construction schedule without tile's multi-step process.

ResultThe finished wet room delivers the designer's vision with none of the maintenance: non-porous glass requires 60–70% less cleaning time and chemicals than tile-and-grout systems, and there's no grout to crack, discolor, or generate builder callbacks. The completed bathroom was later featured in print editorials.

“I will not deal with grout in my shower.”

The homeowner's brief — honored in full

Custom Residential · Shower Enclosures

Privacy that didn't look like a compromise

Boys' Bath — 5-Bedroom New Build, DFW Suburb
Scope
72″ × 90″ opening — 2-panel inline, 28″ pivot door
Systems
3/8″ grey-tinted tempered · dual-swing pivot · matte black hardware
Client
Builder partner
Timeline
1 week, measure to install · 2-day set

ChallengeIn a five-bedroom new build, the boys' rooms shared a bathroom — no ensuite in the plan. The teenage son wanted more privacy than a clear enclosure gives, and his mom brought the request to the build team looking for recommendations, not products.

ApproachWe recommended grey-tinted glass over the usual satin-etch. Same privacy, different message: frosted glass reads as a utility fix, while grey reads as a design decision — edgier, more modern, and right at home with the house's matte black finishes. Fabricated as a two-panel inline enclosure in 3/8-inch tempered grey glass with a 28-inch pivot door that swings both in and out.

ResultSet on the standard one-week measure-to-install timeline, about two days on site. The family loved it — and that word came back through the builder, which counts double.

“Builders don't usually call about a shower. When she called to say the family loved it — that's a big deal.”

Owner, Eridion Glass
