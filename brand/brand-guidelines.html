01 — Foundations

The green rule

The two greens never appear in the same layout. Deep green substitutes for brand green when green has to carry text or contrast — it is not a companion to it. Navy backgrounds use brand green; light backgrounds use deep green. Pick one per layout and commit.

Do — one green, navy carries the rest A brand-green badge with a navy-underlined link. One point of green emphasis; navy does the structural work.
Don't — both greens in one layout A brand-green badge next to a deep-green underlined link. Two greens compete and the emphasis is lost.

Typography

Jost carries the headings — light, geometric, architectural, in the Futura tradition that has defined "modern" for a century. Inter does everything from the lead down: a quiet, legible workhorse that never competes. Two characterful lines of hierarchy, the rest invisible.

01 — Foundations

Voice

Eridion sounds like a master tradesperson talking to another professional — precise, confident, and free of fluff. The craft comes through in specifics, not adjectives.

Principles

  • Builder-to-builder. Write to GCs and builders as peers in the trade — plain, direct, knowledgeable. Skip the consumer-marketing gloss.
  • Specifics over adjectives. "Low-iron glass, field-verified, set to spec" earns more trust than "premium quality." The detail is the proof.
  • Confident, never boastful. State what you do and let the work carry the weight. Hype words undercut it.
  • Calm. Quiet authority — the unhurried tone of someone who's done this a thousand times. Say enough to be clear and reassuring, never so much that you lose, bore, or confuse the listener.

01 — Foundations

Photography

A clean break from generic stock: shoot your own work, in real light. Authentic craft beats any catalogue image.

  • Subject. Finished installs in real homes, panels being fabricated and handled in the shop, and tight detail on edges, joints, and hardware. The crew in-frame sometimes — candid, working, not posed.
  • Light. Natural daylight. Let glass behave like glass — reflections, transparency, clean highlights. No harsh flash.
  • Composition. Architectural and deliberate — strong lines, right angles, symmetry. The geometry should rhyme with the mark. Get close; detail sells craft.
  • Treatment. True-to-life color, crisp, barely filtered. Never moody, HDR, or heavily graded.
  • Avoid. Generic stock, handshake clichés, cluttered frames, anything that could belong to any glass company.

Library to build

  • Hero installs (finished, in the home)
  • In-progress fabrication in the shop
  • Macro detail — edges, joints, hardware
  • Hands at work
  • Before / after pairs
01 — Foundations

Icons & illustration

Icons carry the logo's geometry right down to 24px: squared joins, never rounded.

  • Uniform 2px stroke on a 24px grid · miter joins, square caps.
  • Navy is the default color; brand green only as an accent or active state (one green, never both).
  • 45° angles where an icon naturally allows it · minimal detail.
  • Spot illustrations follow the same logic — flat, geometric, line-based.
